➢ Limitation & existing models
   o Black-Scholes: No jumps, no volatility clustering; short-term skew unexplained.
   o Merton: Jump intensity constant; frequency independent of market state.
   o Heston / Stochastic Volatility: Captures volatility clustering but leaves 𝜆 static;
        extreme tail risk underestimated.
   o Bates / SVJD: Adds jumps to SV framework, yet jump arrival rate remains
        exogenous and fixed.
